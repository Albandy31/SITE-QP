Why managing a Montreal condo is different

From one neighbourhood to the next, Montreal condos are not managed the same way. The age of the building, its size and its history change maintenance priorities, the budget and the contingency fund.

Plateau-Mont-Royal, Rosemont, Villeray: plexes and older small buildings

These neighbourhoods have many plexes and older small buildings, sometimes converted from rentals. With few units, every expense matters, and board members often carry the management alone. The priority is to plan major work such as the roof, masonry or plumbing without surprises for co-owners.

Griffintown, downtown, Quartier des spectacles: towers and newer buildings

In large newer buildings, the challenge is mostly volume: many co-owners, shared equipment, elevators, garages and mechanical systems to maintain. Clear communication and well-monitored maintenance contracts make a real difference.

Côte-des-Neiges, Ahuntsic, Saint-Laurent, Verdun: buildings of all ages

You will find both small condos and mid-size buildings from different eras. The older a building gets, the more useful the maintenance log and the contingency fund study become to plan major work and spread its cost.

Harsh winters add another constraint: a leak or a major breakdown cannot wait until Monday morning. That is why our emergency line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. See our 24/7 emergency service.

What our condo management includes

Our service is built on four pillars, detailed on our Services page: administrative and legal (meetings, registers, insurance, annual REQ filings), financial and accounting (budget, fee collection, arrears follow-up, financial statements, contingency fund), technical and maintenance (maintenance log, contracts, tenders, work supervision, 24/7 emergencies) and communication with co-owners, supported by a software platform.

Bill 16 compliance

Bill 16 gives every condo corporation until August 15, 2028 to adopt a maintenance log and obtain its first contingency fund study, and the corporation's certificate has been mandatory for every unit sale since 2025. We track these deadlines for your corporation. Read our Bill 16 guide.
