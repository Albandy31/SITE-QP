Pillar 4 · Communication

Communication and governance in your condominium.

A platform for everyone, replies within 24 hours and support for the board: the transparency that prevents conflicts.

Request my free quote →

Updated: November 12, 2026

In a condominium, most tensions do not come from the decisions themselves, but from how they are explained. Work announced at the last minute, a fee increase without justification or a request left unanswered is enough to create distrust. Our communication pillar keeps everyone informed and gives the board of directors the means to govern well.

What our communication pillar includes

Software platform

The board of directors and all owners have access to an integrated platform to view the corporation's documents and submit their requests.

Requests and complaints: reply within 24 hours

We handle residents' requests and complaints, by email or through the platform, with a reply guaranteed within 24 hours.

Proactive communication

We communicate regularly with all owners: upcoming work, reminders, board decisions. Information given in advance is better than an explanation after the fact.

Transparency and participation

We encourage owner participation and transparent decisions, in particular by preparing clear documents for meetings.

Mediation

When a conflict arises between owners, we help defuse it and find a solution before it escalates.

Why it matters

  • Fewer conflicts: informed owners challenge less and participate more.
  • A relieved board: directors no longer act as customer service in the evening and on weekends.
  • Calmer meetings: sensitive topics are explained beforehand, not discovered on the spot.
