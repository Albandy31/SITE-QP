Technical management and condo maintenance.

Maintenance log, visits, contracts, work and 24/7 emergencies: we maintain your building to avoid bad surprises.

Updated: November 12, 2026

A well-maintained building costs less than one repaired in a hurry. Our technical pillar organizes maintenance over the long term, follows up on contracts and work, and responds to emergencies day and night. Since Bill 16, this planning is no longer optional: a maintenance log is mandatory for every condominium.

What our technical management includes

Maintenance log and Bill 16

We coordinate periodic inspections and keep the mandatory maintenance log. For the first Bill 16 study, we prepare the documentation and the tender to choose the professional who will produce the maintenance log and the contingency fund study, subject to the board's approval. We then keep the log up to date as work is done.

Building visits

We inspect the common areas twice a year, with a written report and recommendations to fix small problems before they become big ones.

Maintenance contracts

We negotiate and supervise recurring contracts: snow removal, landscaping, cleaning, mechanical systems and elevators.

Work

For minor and major work, inside and outside, we launch the tenders, help the board choose the contractors and supervise the work.

Safety

We have the smoke alarms and other safety systems inspected every year.

24/7 emergencies and claims

Our emergency line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for any major failure: water damage, heating failure, elevator breakdown. We immediately coordinate contractors and insurance claims. See our 24/7 emergency service.

Why it matters

  • Fewer emergencies: many failures give warning signs, and regular visits catch them in time.
  • Better prices: comparable tenders and negotiated contracts avoid overpaying in a rush.
  • A building that keeps its value: an up-to-date maintenance log reassures buyers, their notaries and insurers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about technical management.

How often do you visit the building?+
We inspect the common areas twice a year, with a written report and recommendations.
Do you prepare the maintenance log yourselves?+
The first maintenance log and contingency fund study must be produced by an independent professional, such as an engineer or an architect. We prepare the documentation and the tender to choose them, then keep the log up to date.
Do you manage tenders for work?+
Yes. We launch the tenders, help the board of directors choose the contractors and supervise minor and major work.
What happens in an emergency at night?+
Our emergency line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We dispatch the right contractor and coordinate the insurance claim.
