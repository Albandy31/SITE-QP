Bill 16 timeline: the obligations of Quebec condo syndicates

November 12, 2026  ·  7-min read  ·  By Guillaume Prentki

Adopted in 2019, Bill 16 has been fully in force since August 14, 2025, along with a regulation that sets out the details. Some obligations already apply; others have a 2028 deadline; others come back every year or every five years. Here is the timeline, step by step, so you know what your syndicate must do and when.

Key takeaways
  • Since August 14, 2025, the syndicate must provide the certificate on the condition of the co-ownership within 15 days of a request, with no grace period.
  • Existing syndicates have until August 15, 2028 to obtain their maintenance log and their contingency fund study.
  • After the study, the board of directors sets the new contribution within 30 days after the annual meeting, with up to 10 years to catch up on an underfunded reserve.
  • After that, the board updates the log every year and a professional reviews it every 5 years (or every 10 years for some small buildings); the study is redone every 5 years.

The timeline at a glance

  • 2019: Bill 16 is adopted.
  • After August 14, 2023: a log or study obtained from that date from an eligible, independent professional is recognized and remains valid for 5 years.
  • July 15, 2025: the date that determines which rules apply to your syndicate (under the co-owners' control or not).
  • August 14, 2025: the law and its regulation are fully in force; the syndicate's certificate becomes mandatory.
  • August 15, 2028: deadline for existing syndicates' first maintenance log and first contingency fund study.
  • Every year: the board updates the log and presents contributions at the annual meeting.
  • Every 5 years: a new contingency fund study and a professional review of the log (10 years for some small buildings).

Since August 14, 2025: what already applies

The certificate on the condition of the co-ownership

The syndicate must give a co-owner who asks for it, within 15 days, a certificate on the condition of the co-ownership. The seller must then give it to the buyer in good time, usually around the promise to purchase. The law provides no grace period: even without a contingency fund study, the certificate must be completed with all the other information available. The Société d'habitation du Québec provides a template.

The certificate includes, among other things, the contingency fund balance, contributions for the last three years, the surplus or deficit in the last three financial statements, the current year's budget, the self-insurance fund and the highest deductible, inspections, claims and work in the last five years, work planned for the next ten years, and pending lawsuits.

A minimum contingency fund contribution until the study is done

Until it obtains the study, the syndicate must pay into the contingency fund at least:

  • 5% of the contributions to common expenses, if the transition meeting was held before July 15, 2025;
  • 0.5% of the building's reconstruction value, if it was held after July 15, 2025.

By August 15, 2028: the maintenance log and the contingency fund study

All divided co-ownerships are covered, small or large, residential or commercial. Syndicates whose building was under the co-owners' control on July 15, 2025 have until August 15, 2028 to obtain their first maintenance log and first contingency fund study.

If your syndicate already has these documents, they are recognized if they were obtained after August 14, 2023 from an eligible professional (engineer, chartered appraiser, architect or professional technologist whose work is mainly in real estate) with no connection to the co-ownership. They then remain valid for five years. A document obtained before August 15, 2023 must be redone before August 15, 2028.

Mind the timelines

Three years sounds like a lot, but the contingency fund study is based on the maintenance log, and eligible professionals are in high demand. Allow several months between the first quote and the final documents.

What about new condos?

For buildings where control passed from the developer to the co-owners between July 15 and November 12, 2025, the developer must provide the maintenance log and the study within 6 months after the transition meeting.

After the study: adjusting contributions

At the annual meeting following receipt of the study, the board of directors presents to the co-owners, for consultation, the required contribution based on the professional's recommendations. Co-owners give their opinion, but the decision belongs to the board, which sets the contribution within 30 days after the meeting. If the fund is far behind, the catch-up can be spread over 10 years: the professional takes this into account in the recommendations.

After that: recurring obligations

  • Every year, the board of directors updates the maintenance log with new information and must justify any required work that was not done.
  • Within 60 days of receiving it, the log is filed in the register and made available to co-owners (or given out before the annual meeting if it falls within that period).
  • Every 5 years, a new contingency fund study is obtained and a professional reviews the log.
  • Every 10 years only, for the log, if the building has no more than 8 private portions (not counting storage and parking), has no common portion inside a building, or has no more than 3 storeys entirely above ground.

Where to start in 2026

  1. Prepare the certificate now: gather financial statements, insurance policies, inspection reports, claims and work history. A request can come at any time.
  2. Check the minimum contribution to the contingency fund while you wait for the study.
  3. Gather the building's documentation: plans, warranties, maintenance contracts, work invoices, minutes.
  4. Ask for quotes for the maintenance log and the study, ideally in the same mandate.
  5. Plan the annual meeting that will follow receipt of the study, to present the new contributions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Bill 16 deadline for existing condos?

Syndicates whose building was under the co-owners' control on July 15, 2025 have until August 15, 2028 to obtain their first maintenance log and their first contingency fund study.

Is the syndicate's certificate already mandatory?

Yes. Since August 14, 2025, the syndicate must provide the certificate on the condition of the co-ownership within 15 days of a co-owner's request, with no grace period, even if it does not yet have its contingency fund study.

How much must go into the contingency fund until the study is done?

At least 5% of the contributions to common expenses if the transition meeting was held before July 15, 2025, or 0.5% of the building's reconstruction value if it was held after.
