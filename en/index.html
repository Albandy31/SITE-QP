Bill 16: are you compliant?

Bill 16 imposes new obligations on all condominium corporations in Quebec. At Quatre Piliers, we guide you through every step.

Mandatory maintenance logCorporations must produce a maintenance log by August 15, 2028.
Contingency fund studyCorporations have until August 15, 2028 to obtain their first contingency fund study.
Corporation status certificateRequired at every unit sale since 2025.
