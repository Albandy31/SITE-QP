Phased condominiums and horizontal management

When a condominium is built in phases, each building can have its own corporation, linked to an initial corporation that manages the common areas of the whole development. This requires separate budgets and registers, a correct allocation of shared expenses and coordinated decisions. Our Custom plan includes this horizontal management of several corporations.

Major work, infiltration and Bill 16

Water infiltration, French drains and foundations are among the most expensive work a corporation faces. We coordinate inspections, tenders, professionals and work supervision. Bill 16 gives every condo corporation until August 15, 2028 to adopt a maintenance log and obtain its first contingency fund study, and the corporation's certificate has been mandatory for every unit sale since 2025. These tools help plan this work instead of reacting to it. Read our Bill 16 guide.

What our condo management includes

Our service is built on four pillars, detailed on our Services page: administrative and legal (meetings, registers, insurance, annual REQ filings), financial and accounting (budget, fee collection, arrears follow-up, financial statements, contingency fund), technical and maintenance (maintenance log, contracts, tenders, work supervision, 24/7 emergencies) and communication with co-owners, supported by a software platform.
