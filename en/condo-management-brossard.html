Managing a condo in Brossard: what changes with the building

New buildings: a good start after the developer

When the developer hands control over to the co-owners, everything has to be set up: budget, maintenance contracts, insurance, registers and follow-up of construction deficiencies during the first years. Under Bill 16, the obligations for the maintenance log and the contingency fund study depend on the date of the transition meeting: our Bill 16 timeline explains how.

Older buildings: planning major work

Roof, windows, masonry, parking: as buildings age, major expenses come up. The maintenance log and the contingency fund study help you plan them and spread their cost. If the fund is behind, the law allows a 10-year catch-up.

Communication in English and French

Brossard co-owners come from many backgrounds. We communicate with them in English and French, and our online platform gives them access to the syndicate's documents at any time.

What our condo management includes

  • Administrative and legal: meetings, board of directors, registers, declaration of co-ownership, insurance.
  • Financial and accounting: budget, collection of condo fees, arrears, financial statements, contingency fund.
  • Technical and maintenance: maintenance log, contracts, tenders, work follow-up, 24/7 emergencies.
  • Communication and governance: request follow-up, notices to co-owners, online platform.

How much does it cost?

Our indicative plans are Essential at $25 per unit per month, for small syndicates of fewer than 10 units, and Serenity at $35 per unit per month, with a dedicated manager and a software platform. The Custom plan lets you choose services à la carte. The rate per unit decreases with the size of the building: see our pricing page.

The transition to Quatre Piliers usually takes 15 to 30 days. See our guide: how to switch condo managers.

Bill 16 applies here too

Existing syndicates have until August 15, 2028 to obtain a maintenance log and their first contingency fund study, and the syndicate's certificate has been mandatory for every unit sale since 2025. See our Bill 16 timeline or our complete Bill 16 guide.
