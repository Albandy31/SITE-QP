Small condominiums have obligations too

Even a three-unit corporation must keep its registers, call an annual meeting, manage a contingency fund and comply with Bill 16. Bill 16 gives every condo corporation until August 15, 2028 to adopt a maintenance log and obtain its first contingency fund study, and the corporation's certificate has been mandatory for every unit sale since 2025. Our Essential plan is designed for these small corporations. Read our Bill 16 guide.
